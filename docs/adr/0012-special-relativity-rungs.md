# Special-relativity rung definitions

## Decision Outcome
Two SR rungs, both shown with caveats:
* **SR-a**: the Hamiltonian H = γmc² − μm/r (relativistic kinetic energy in a Newtonian potential). Per orbit 2π[(1−μ²/c²h²)^{-1/2} − 1] ≈ GR/6 = **7.1634″/cy**. Documented as a *model* result. Other SR couplings give 1/3 or 0 (Lemmon & Mondragon v6; Trump & Schieve).
* **SR-b**: "velocity-dependent 1PN terms only", **always labelled with its gauge**: 7/6 GR = 50.144″/cy (PPN isotropic/harmonic) or 5/6 GR = 35.817″/cy (Schwarzschild areal). Presented as teaching material showing why the SR/GR split is not observable.
