      What you are looking at

      The measurement

      The engine integrates the Sun, Mercury and whichever bodies are switched on, from the at 2440400.5 (1969-06-28). At each it records the on the (ADR-0011). A of those longitudes against time gives the advance rate in , with a . The thin wiggly line is the , which swings by tens of arcseconds with the planets' ; it is drawn, not fitted.

      Short spans give wobbly rates: the periodic perturbations do not average out. Park's literature numbers use a 2000-year window; for a comparable figure run 1000 years or more.

      Planets (Newtonian pull)

      Each planet's tugs Mercury's orbit around. Removing a planet from the integration is “ set to zero” (ADR-0014 ). Venus (≈277 ″/cy), Jupiter (≈154) and the Earth–Moon barycentre (≈91) dominate; Saturn adds ≈7, Mars ≈2.5, Uranus and Neptune a fraction of an arcsecond. Together ≈532 ″/cy, the part Le Verrier could explain.

      General relativity

      The Schwarzschild field of the Sun adds 6πGM/(c²a(1−e²)) per orbit: ≈42.98 ″/cy, the famous . With it scales as (2+2γ−β)/3. The “” option applies 1PN among all bodies instead of the Sun alone (IAS15 only). WHFast uses a that reproduces the same secular advance, only for β = γ = 1.

      Solar J2 and Lense–Thirring

      The Sun is slightly oblate ( ≈ 2.2×10⁻⁷), adding ≈0.03 ″/cy. Its spin drags inertial frames (), about −0.002 ″/cy. Both are tiny: on 300–400-year runs the is still at or above the J2 effect and far above Lense–Thirring, so those rows are usually greyed. The J2 and spin overrides scale the real physics and mark the run non-nominal; the display exaggeration only changes the drawing.

      Asteroids

      The first N numbered of DE440 (default 16) as point masses. Park finds ≈0.001 ″/cy for 343 of them, below what the viewer can resolve on spans under about 1000 years.

      Rung lab

      Mercury alone around a fixed Sun, to compare central-force models: Newton (0), special relativity in two ( 7.16, 50.14 or 35.82 ″/cy) and GR (42.98). The SR rungs show how much a “relativistic” label alone fixes; the split between SR and GR parts depends on the gauge and is not observable (ADR-0012).
